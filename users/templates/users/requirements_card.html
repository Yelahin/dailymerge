Source Requirements

To ensure articles are uploaded correctly, please follow these guidelines:

  • Valid RSS URL: Provide a direct link to the source's RSS feed.
  • Standard Format: The feed should follow standard RSS 2.0.
  • Content Quality: Feeds should contain clear titles, summaries, and publication dates.
  • Image Support: For best visual results, feeds should include media tags for article images.
  • Category Assignment: Choose or create a category that best describes the content of this source.

New articles will be automatically synchronized based on your profile settings.
